# Hexenbane Private Playtest Agreement

This agreement is between you (the "Tester") and the developer of Hexenbane (the "Developer"). It covers your access to private, pre-release builds of Hexenbane (the "Build"). Please read it in full. By ticking the agreement box on the application form, you confirm that you have read it and agree to follow it if you are given access.

## 1. The Build is confidential

The Build is unreleased and confidential. Everything in it, including its story, characters, cards, art, music, systems and anything you learn about the game through the playtest, is private until the Developer makes it public.

## 2. Your access is personal

Access is given to you alone. You may not transfer, sell, lend or give your access, download links, keys or account to anyone else.

## 3. Do not share or redistribute the Build

You agree not to:

- upload, mirror or redistribute the Build or any part of it, anywhere;
- share the Build, its download links or its files with any other person, even a friend you trust;
- extract, copy or redistribute any of the game's assets, such as art, audio, music, text or data files.

## 4. Do not publish anything without permission

Unless the Developer gives you permission in writing (a Discord message from the Developer counts), you agree not to:

- stream the Build publicly;
- publish gameplay videos or clips of the Build;
- publish screenshots of the Build;
- publish unreleased story, lore, card, system or other game information.

Sharing inside the private playtest channels of the Hexenbane Discord server is allowed. If you are unsure whether something is allowed, ask first.

## 5. No reverse engineering

You agree not to decompile, disassemble, data-mine or otherwise reverse engineer the Build, except where the law that applies to you does not allow this restriction.

## 6. Ownership

Hexenbane and everything in it, including its code, art, audio, music, writing, characters, names and designs, belongs to its respective owner or owners. This agreement does not transfer any ownership of, or rights in, Hexenbane or its intellectual property to you.

## 7. Feedback

Giving feedback is voluntary. If you give feedback, ideas or bug reports, the Developer may use them freely to improve Hexenbane and its future versions, without owing you payment or credit. Your feedback does not give you any ownership of the game.

## 8. The Build is unfinished

The Build is a work in progress. It may contain bugs, crashes, missing or placeholder content, unbalanced systems and features that change or disappear. It is provided as it is, without any promise that it will work correctly. Save data may be lost between builds.

## 9. Access can end

The Developer may change, pause or end the playtest, or revoke your access, at any time and for any reason. If your access ends, you agree to delete your copies of the Build.

## 10. Changes to this agreement

The Developer may publish a new version of this agreement for future builds. Each version has its own version identifier, and you may be asked to accept a new version to keep testing.
