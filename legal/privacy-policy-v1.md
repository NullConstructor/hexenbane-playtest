# Hexenbane Playtest Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the developer of Hexenbane (the "Developer") collects during the private playtest, why, where it is kept and how to have it removed. It covers the playtest application form and the playtest builds of the game (the "Build"). It does not cover Discord, which has its own privacy policy.

## 1. What we collect

**When you apply.** The answers on the application form: your display name, Discord username, an optional backup email, your answers about yourself and games, your PC's CPU, GPU, RAM and operating system, and the version of the Playtest Agreement you accepted, with the time you accepted it.

**Gameplay data (telemetry).** Only if you say yes when the Build first asks (or later in Settings), it sends short records of what happens in the game, for example: a Hunt started with a coven and two Implements, a fight won or lost against a named quarry with the Beats, Vitality and cards it took, a card reward offered and the card you took, a purchase, a Hunt's end. Each record carries the Build's version, a random install ID created on your PC, a random session ID and the time. Telemetry does not include your name, Discord username, email, IP address in the stored record, files from your PC or anything you type.

**Crash reports.** Only if you said yes to gameplay data: when the Build crashes, it notices the next time you start it and sends a crash report; when it hits an error while you play, it sends an error report. A report holds the error, where in the game's code it happened, the last lines of the game's log, the Build's version, your operating system, graphics card and window size, where you were in the game, and the same random install ID. The game's log can include the path of the game's data folder on your PC, which may contain your Windows user name.

**Reports you send (F8).** When you press F8 and choose Send, the Build sends what you wrote, and, if you leave those boxes ticked, a screenshot of the game and the last lines of the game's log. It adds the Build's version, your operating system, graphics card and window size, and where you are in the Hunt (coven, Night, Hour, Vitality, current quarry). The report is posted in the private #bug-reports channel of the Hexenbane Discord server, where the Developer and other playtesters can see it. The Build also keeps a copy on your PC, in the game's own data folder.

## 2. Why we collect it

Only to run the playtest and make Hexenbane better:

- to choose testers and contact them (applications);
- to find out which quarries, bosses, cards, Implements and systems need tuning, and where players get stuck or stop (telemetry);
- to find and fix crashes and bugs (crash reports and F8 reports).

We do not sell any of it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone other than the services below.

## 3. Where it is kept

- **Supabase** stores applications, telemetry and crash reports.
- **Discord** carries F8 reports, and the first report of each new kind of crash, in the private #bug-reports channel.

These services handle the data for us under their own terms and may store it outside your country. Your IP address is seen by these services when the Build connects to them, as with any internet connection, but we do not store it with your telemetry.

## 4. How long we keep it

Telemetry and crash reports are kept until 12 months after the playtest ends, then deleted or reduced to totals that cannot be linked to an install. Applications are kept while the playtest runs, and deleted on request. F8 reports stay in #bug-reports until they are no longer useful.

## 5. Your choices

- **Choose.** The Build asks before it sends any gameplay data or crash reports. Settings has a Playtest Data switch to change your answer at any time. When it is off, the Build sends neither, and drops any it was still waiting to send. F8 reports are only ever sent when you press Send.
- **See your install ID.** Settings shows your install ID, so you can tell us which data is yours.
- **Have it deleted.** Message the Developer on the Hexenbane Discord server with your install ID (for gameplay data and crash reports) or your application ID, and we will delete it. You can delete your own F8 posts in #bug-reports.

## 6. Age

The playtest is for people aged 16 or older. If we learn that a tester is younger, we will remove their data and access.

## 7. Changes

If we change what the Build collects, we will publish a new version of this policy with a new version identifier and announce it in #playtest-announcements before the change ships.

## 8. Contact

Questions about this policy: message the Developer on the Hexenbane Discord server.
